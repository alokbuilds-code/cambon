# CHANEL — DIGITAL CONCEPT
## Haute Couture Digital Art Direction & Architectural Specification

This digital experience represents an unofficial creative concept and cinematic fashion exhibition inspired by the Parisian heritage, restraint, and graphic power associated with CHANEL.

---

### Creative Direction & Core WOW Moments
1. **The 5-Second Title Opening Sequence (Section 03):**
   * Black screen → Tactile grain → CHANEL enters via blurred letter-spacing expansion → Atmospheric screen breath glow → Photographic fragments cut in → Typography recedes into depth → Vertical and horizontal screen split → Autumn/Winter campaign hero settles.
2. **Layered Depth Typography (Section 04):**
   * Multi-layer typographic depth (`PARIS` / `CAMBON`) floating behind couture silhouettes and moving at differential parallax speeds.
3. **Continuous Moving Image Strip (Section 24):**
   * Infinite smooth cinematic marquee showcasing Métiers d'Art atelier moments (Lesage embroidery, saddlery stitching, silk draping, pearl setting).
4. **Scroll-Based Physical Transformation (Section 08 & 26):**
   * Pinned viewport composition that morphs from an intimate framed piece (`48vw / 60vh`) to a sweeping fullscreen vista (`94vw / 90vh`) with un-zooming camera work.
5. **Fullscreen Pure Typography Moment (Section 09):**
   * Strips away all UI into pure black silence: massive, slow-drifting `CHANEL` and italic `Paris`.
6. **The Silence Section — Le Silence de Rue Cambon (Section 20 & 21):**
   * Warm architectural cream sanctuary creating emotional breathing room between high-motion sequences.
7. **Dual-Image Hover Crossfades (Section 16):**
   * Product cards seamlessly cross-fade from primary angle to secondary silhouette on mouseenter with 3D perspective tilt.
8. **Signature Horizontal Footwear Runway (Section 25 & 26):**
   * Sticky viewport pinned with GSAP ScrollTrigger gliding footwear concepts across parallax background typography.
9. **Interactive Three.js Sculpture — L’Harmonie Géométrique (Sections 32 & 33):**
   * Polished obsidian black lacquer and brushed pale gold interlocking ribbons in slow continuous rotation, governed by an IntersectionObserver for peak 60fps performance.
10. **The Final Film Frame Footer (Section 19):**
    * Closing cinematic vignette with generative Web Audio ambient sound synthesizer and colossal `CHANEL / PARIS` typography.
